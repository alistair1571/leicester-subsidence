Subsidence repair costs

Rough UK price ranges for each stage, and the things that push a job up or down.

Send a free enquiry How this service works

Subsidence costs vary enormously, because every house and every cause is different. The figures below are broad ranges drawn from published UK guides and vary between sources. They are not quotes. Actual prices depend on the property, access, the firm and how much work is needed. If you have buildings insurance, the insurer normally pays for investigation and repair after your excess.

Typical ranges

ItemTypical range
Engineer's report or survey£500 to £1,500
Crack monitoring£500 to £2,000
CCTV drain survey£250 to £600
Crack stitching or repair£1,000 to £3,000
Resin injection£5,000 to £15,000
Traditional underpinning, per metreabout £1,500 to £2,600
Underpinning a typical house£10,000 to £30,000, and more in complex cases (£50,000 or above)
Tree workA few hundred to a few thousand pounds

What affects the price

  • The cause: a drain repair or tree work costs less than foundations
  • The extent: how much of the house is affected
  • Ground conditions and depth to firm soil
  • Access for machinery, and whether you share walls with neighbours
  • Redecoration, flooring and other finishes after the structural work
  • Professional fees, building control and any party wall costs

Insurance numbers for context

Association of British Insurers figures reported the average subsidence claim at about £20,000 in the second quarter of 2026, and record total payouts of around £307m for 2025. Individual claims vary widely, from a few thousand pounds to six figures.

Ways to avoid overpaying

  1. Establish the cause first.Do not agree to foundation work before a survey, drain check and monitoring where needed.
  2. Get written, itemised quotes from more than one firm, and check they cover the same scope.
  3. Check insurance first.If you are covered, work agreed by your insurer is paid for by them.
  4. Ask about guarantees and whether they transfer to a buyer.
  5. Be wary of pressure to sign on the day, or offers of a "free survey" that leads straight to an expensive repair.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to fix subsidence?

From a few hundred pounds for a drain survey to tens of thousands for underpinning. The cause and extent decide the cost.

Does insurance pay for underpinning?

Usually yes if the claim is accepted, less your excess.

Is the first survey free?

Sometimes firms offer a free initial visit. Make sure you understand what is free and what comes next.
