Subsidence in Spinney Hills

Spinney Hills is a dense area of Victorian terraces east of the city centre, with an unusual piece of local history: a reported collapse of an old shaft.

Send a free enquiry How this service works

Spinney Hills, north-east of the city centre, is characterised by tightly packed Victorian terraces, a lively mix of shops and a high proportion of rented homes. It also has a reported history of old ground workings.

The ground and the houses

One reported local example is the collapse of a shaft dug in the 1700s in search of coal, which opened a hole several metres across. It is a reminder that old, forgotten workings can sit under built-up ground, although most subsidence in the area is still more likely to come from clay, drains or trees. If cracks appear suddenly, with no seasonal pattern, ask a specialist whether voids should be considered. See our page on mining and ground voids.

This is a general picture of the area, not a survey. Ground and construction can change from one street to the next, and only an inspection of your own house can show what is happening. The British Geological Survey maps are free to check; see Clay and Ground Under Leicester Houses.

What tends to matter in Spinney Hills

  • Old shafts and filled ground: check mining and local authority records for your address
  • Drains under tightly packed terraces
  • Landlords and tenants dealing with cracks in rented homes
  • Sudden ground movement, which is different from the gradual seasonal pattern of clay

Which council and who deals with drains

Spinney Hills is in the city of Leicester, so Leicester City Council deals with its street and park trees and housing enforcement. For the council's role, read council trees and subsidence in Leicester. Sewers and drains beyond your property boundary are normally the water company's responsibility, which for most of Leicestershire is Severn Trent Water; drains inside your boundary serving only your home are usually yours. See Leaking Drains and Washed-Out Ground.

Postcodes

Spinney Hills is mainly in the LE5 postcode area. If you are not sure whether we cover your street, send an enquiry anyway.

If you see cracks

  1. Measure and photograph every crack with a date, and do not fill it. See Reading Cracks in Your Walls.
  2. Tell your buildings insurer promptly if you have cover. See Claiming on Buildings Insurance.
  3. Look at trees and drains close to the cracks.
  4. Send an enquiry if you want one independent firm covering Spinney Hills to look at it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there old mining under Spinney Hills?

There is a reported historic shaft collapse in the area, but that does not mean your street is affected. Check the mining and ground records for your address and ask a specialist if you see sudden movement.
